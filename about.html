About me

Hi, I'm Blaine Wood. Photography started as a hobby and turned into the way I notice the world. I shoot the people, places, and things I come across: street scenes, portraits, the light around us, and the small details most people walk past.

I'm based in Atlanta, GA, and I shoot with a Leica M11. What draws me to a scene is light and shadow, people with a positive energy, and the gestures that make up candid moments.

Every print is made to order on archival paper so it will last for decades.
